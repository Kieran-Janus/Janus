---
description: Make a skin's pieces move or glow, or give it a walk style. Usage: /animate-skin <skin Id or name> <what you want, e.g. "flap the wings slowly and make it walk like a zombie">
---

Animate a skin: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read `CLAUDE.md`; `docs/CUSTOMIZE.md` > "Change and animate skins" (its tables "Every animation effect" and "Every walk style"), "Animated skins", "Animate parts of your own model" and "Walk styles"; the effect list at the top of `src/shared/SkinAnimate.luau`; `src/shared/Config/SkinAnimate.luau` (the `Rarity` limits and each effect's `Presets` numbers) and `src/shared/Config/SkinMotion.luau`.
2. Find the skin in `src/shared/Config/Skins/<Theme>.luau` by its `Id` or `Name` (the Tormented Tower's skin is in `src/shared/Config/TowerParkour.luau` > `Skin`). If several skins match the name, or none, ask me which one. Read its whole `Style`: which accent patterns it has (only `Wings`, `Tail` and `Cape` can move), its `Head`, `Glow`, `Aura`, `Crazy` and any `Animate` / `Motion` it already has. For `Part:<name>` targets, look up the head piece's part names in its builder in `src/shared/HeadPieces.luau` (or the custom model's parts).
3. Turn what I asked into effects (my words are plain English: "flap" = `Flap`, "spin" = `Spin`, "glow on and off" = `Pulse` or `Flicker`, "change colours" = `ColorCycle`, "sparkle" = `SparkleBurst`, "walk like a zombie" = `Motion = "Zombie"`...), within the skin's rarity (`Config.SkinAnimate.Rarity`: Common none, Uncommon 1, Rare 3, CRAZY 6; a `Motion` walk style only on Rare and CRAZY). If I asked for more than the rarity allows, or for something the skin can't do, say so and pick the best ones. Rules:
   - every `Target` must exist on the skin (Wings needs the Wings pattern, Glow needs a `Glow`, SparkleBurst needs an `Aura`), or the effect does nothing;
   - with `Crazy.Rainbow` no colour effect (`Pulse`, `ColorCycle`, `Flicker`, `Shimmer`) on `Accents`, `All` or a pattern name; with `Crazy.Pulse` no `Pulse` or `Flicker` on the `Glow` (a `ColorCycle` on the `Glow` is fine);
   - keep it calm: one or two moving groups is plenty, `Speed` 0.3 to 2, `SparkleBurst` `Every` 2 or more. Spooky-cute, never scary-gory.
   Don't change the skin's look, `Id`, `Rarity`, crate or price (Common skins never animate: say so and offer a similar Uncommon-or-better skin, or a new one with `/add-skin`). Add `Animate = { ... }` (and `Motion = "..."`) as the last keys of its `Style`, written like the other animated skins (for example `Candy_BubblegumBat`, `Pumpkin_MegaGourdSupreme`), ending the list with `} :: { any }, -- (effects differ in shape)`.
4. If the skin uses a custom model (`assets/Cosmetics/<Id>.rbxm` or one I made in Studio) and I asked to move a part of it, explain the Studio attribute steps from `docs/CUSTOMIZE.md` > "Change and animate skins" > Way 3 instead of writing code (models live in Studio, not in `src/`).
5. Run `stylua src`, `selene src` and `lune run tests/run`: `skins.spec` checks every skin's `Animate` and `Motion` (no errors, no warnings) and `skinanimate.spec` the rarity limits. Fix what they report.
6. Look at it, **only if the preview tooling exists** (the cloud session has it at `/tmp/claude-0/render/export.luau`; on my PC it usually isn't there, then skip this step and rely on step 7): `lune run tests/catwalk -- ids <Id>` and render it as the command prints (pieces at rest). If it stops with "can't find the scene exporter", skip it. The renderer only makes still pictures, so describe the movement in words.
7. Playtest in Studio when it is connected: wear the skin (F8 > "Unlock all items", Menu > Skins; choosing a skin there also takes off any Skin Studio draft you were wearing), walk, jump, dance at the Photo Spot and check the pieces move smoothly and stay attached; open the Shop and check the card's preview moves (PC). Tell me I can fine-tune it by eye in Skin Studio: open the skin, Animate tab, change Speed or Amount with **Wear on me** ticked, Save, then `/save-skins` before Stop.
8. Commit "Animate skin: <name>" (only the files you changed, by name) and push to the current branch (never force-push). Tell me which effects you added, what each looks like, and how to make it faster, slower, bigger or smaller (`Speed`, `Amount`).
